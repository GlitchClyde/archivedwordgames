How to play

Wordle

Goal

  1. Slide to pick from 5 to 16 letters before you start.
  2. Type or tap a real dictionary word, then press Enter.
  3. Find the hidden word in six tries.

Colours

A
Right letter, right spot.
B
The letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
C
The letter is not in the word.

Hard mode

Switch it on at the Wordle screen before you start. Every hint you uncover must be used in your next guess.

Hangman

  1. Slide to pick from 5 to 16 letters.
  2. Tap letters, or type on your keyboard, to uncover the hidden word.
  3. Six wrong letters end the game.

Anagram

  1. Tap or type the letters in order to spell a valid word.
  2. Longer words score more. Hints cost points.
  3. Skipping a word resets your streak.

Word Search

  1. Choose how many words (3 to 15) are hidden. The grid grows with the count.
  2. Drag across the letters, or tap the first and last letter.
  3. Find every word on the list to clear the grid.
